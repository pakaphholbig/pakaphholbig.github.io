04LLM Quantization & Inference EfficiencyProject · MIT 6.5940 TinyML+

I explored 4-bit weights and activations for LLaMA through post-training quantization and quantization-aware training. I also wrote ARM NEON and x86 AVX2 kernels for INT4 matrix multiplication in on-device LLaMA2-7B inference, using vectorized dequantization and multithreading.
