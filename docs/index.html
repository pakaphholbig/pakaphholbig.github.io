My interests

01Alignment & post-training

I’m interested in building data-efficient post-training pipelines to align LLMs with specific use cases. My hands-on research and engineering experience spans synthetic data, reward design, large-scale training environments, evals, and post-training with KD, SFT, and RL.

02Multi-agent systems

I also explore how multi-agent systems solve complex problems and scale with test-time compute. For fun, I built an orchestrator for coding agents to manage multiple sessions, each with its own context, memory, and preferences. Another project explores how agents can simulate the way people work and collaborate in real life.

Always happy to connect and exchange ideas.
